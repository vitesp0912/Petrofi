## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Account and identity information

Depending on how you register or sign in, we may collect:

- Full name  
- Mobile phone number  
- Email address (optional at signup; may be used for email OTP login)  
- Role (e.g. dealer, manager)  
- Authentication identifiers linked to your account  

Phone and/or email OTPs are used to verify sign-in. OTP delivery may involve SMS and email service providers.

### 3.2 Outlet (pump) information

- Outlet / RO name  
- Address, city, state, PIN code  
- Outlet contact phone and email  
- Owner name and phone  
- Registration and subscription status  
- Configuration such as fuel types, shifts, nozzles, tanks, bank accounts, and payment modes  

### 3.3 Operational and financial business data

Data you or your staff enter or generate in PetroFI, which may include:

- Daily meter / nozzle readings and fuel sales  
- Expenses and other transactions  
- Digital sales and payment-mode breakdowns  
- Credit (udhar) customers, credit sales, and repayments  
- Treasury movements, cash/bank balances, money transfers  
- Fuel receipts, tank levels, and inventory purchases/sales  
- Reports you generate (e.g. PDF / Excel downloads)  
- Shift-linked operational records  

This is primarily **business operational data**. Where it includes information about your customers (e.g. credit customer name or phone), you are responsible for having a lawful basis to process that data in PetroFI.

### 3.4 Device, app, and technical data

On the iOS app, the Android app, and the web app, we may collect:

- App or browser version and basic platform information (iOS, Android, or web browser) as needed for updates and support  
- Security session data (authentication tokens used to keep you signed in). On the web app, this may be stored in your browser.  
- Local preferences stored on your device or in your browser for settings and display continuity  

On the iOS and Android apps, we may also collect:

- Device push notification tokens used to deliver alerts  
- Connectivity-related signals used for offline and online handling  

On the web app at app.petrofi.in, we may also collect browser type and basic technical information needed to load and secure the site.  

### 3.5 Audit and diagnostics

- Audit / activity logs of significant actions (e.g. create/update/login-related events) for accountability and support  
- Error and diagnostic logs that may include limited technical details needed to investigate failures  

### 3.6 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not require government ID scans, precise continuous GPS tracking, or payment card PAN storage as part of core PetroFI features, unless you separately upload such material or we introduce a feature with its own notice.

## 8. Outlet data isolation and access control

PetroFI is designed as a **multi-outlet** system. Operational data is generally scoped to your pump/outlet. Access is restricted using authentication and server-side security controls. You must still use strong device security and revoke access for former staff promptly.

## 10. Data retention

We retain information for one year, until you explicitly request that we delete it.

Pending or rejected registrations may be retained for review and fraud prevention. On the iOS and Android apps, device tokens are updated or removed when you sign out, uninstall, or tokens are invalidated, and local data may remain until cleared by the operating system or an app data reset. On the web app, session data in your browser remains until you sign out or clear site data for app.petrofi.in.

When you explicitly request deletion, we will delete or anonymise personal data we control, except where retention is required or permitted by law.

## 11. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the Service, which may include:

- Encrypted transport (HTTPS / TLS)  
- Token-based authentication sessions

- Audit logging of sensitive actions  

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. You must protect OTP-capable devices and not share OTPs.
