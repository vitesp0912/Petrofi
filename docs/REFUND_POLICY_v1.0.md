## 1. Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy governs subscriptions to PetroFI, a petrol pump management software product owned and provided by **Vitespace Private Limited** (“Vitespace”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). PetroFI is the product name.

By purchasing a PetroFI subscription, you agree to this policy, along with our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Subscription cancellation

You may cancel your PetroFI subscription at any time by emailing admin@petrofi.in.

Cancellation takes effect when PetroFI confirms it. That confirmation stops any future renewal.

- Cancellation does not automatically result in a refund. Refund eligibility is determined under this policy.  
- Where a refund is made, access to PetroFI for that pump ends after the refund.  

---

## 3. 15-day refund guarantee

PetroFI offers a 15-calendar-day refund guarantee on the first paid subscription for a pump. The subscription start date in India Standard Time (IST) counts as Day 1. The refund request must be received by email by 11:59 PM IST on the 15th calendar day.

To qualify:

- The refund request must be received by email by 11:59 PM IST on that 15th calendar day.  
- The request must relate to the first paid subscription for that pump. The guarantee is one per pump.  
- That pump’s first paid subscription must not already have been refunded under this guarantee.  

A request received by that deadline remains eligible if the review is completed after Day 15. After the refund is made, access for that pump ends.

Eligible customers will receive a full refund of the subscription fee paid, including applicable GST, subject to legally required tax adjustments.

PetroFI does not change the plan while a paid period is still running. The guarantee does not apply to a later renewal. At renewal, the pump can choose any available plan, and that payment is a new subscription period.

---

## 4. Refunds after the 15-day period

After 11:59 PM IST on that 15th calendar day:

- Subscription fees are generally non-refundable.  
- Early cancellation of a prepaid subscription does not entitle the customer to a refund for the unused portion of the subscription period.  
- Renewal payments are not refundable merely because the customer cancels after renewal.  

Exceptions may apply where required by applicable law, including duplicate payments, erroneous charges, or material failure to provide the subscribed service.

---

## 5. Duplicate payments and billing errors

If you are charged more than once for the same subscription, or you are charged an incorrect amount, email us the payment reference at admin@petrofi.in.

A verified duplicate payment is refunded. It is not kept as extra subscription time. A verified billing error is corrected or refunded.

If a payment is taken but the subscription is not activated, PetroFI will verify the payment. After that verification, PetroFI will activate the subscription or initiate a refund within 15 calendar days.

## 7. Refund request procedure

To request a refund:

1. Email admin@petrofi.in.  
2. Provide your registered account details, pump name, payment reference, and refund request.  
3. PetroFI will review your request and notify you of its decision.  

We may request reasonable information necessary to verify the payment and account ownership.

---

## 8. Refund processing

We will approve or decline a refund within 7 calendar days of receiving the refund request.

Where a refund is approved, PetroFI will initiate the refund within 15 calendar days of that approval.

Refunds will generally be returned to the original payment method. The time taken for the funds to appear in your account may depend on your bank, payment provider, or card issuer.

PetroFI does not guarantee a specific bank processing time.

## 9. Subscription renewal

PetroFI does not renew a subscription automatically and does not take automatic deductions. The plan is not changed while a paid period is still running. Subscription fees are payable in advance for the billing period you choose. You renew by starting a payment yourself, and at that time you may choose any available plan. You may also choose not to renew.

The billing period and amount are shown before you pay. Renewal and price changes are also described in the Terms of Service.
